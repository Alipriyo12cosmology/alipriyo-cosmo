Beyond research

Football is a passion.

From the roar of Old Trafford to the pride and tradition of Mohun Bagan, football has a special place in my life. It is more than a game: it is about history, emotion, loyalty, and unforgettable moments.

A little about my football world

As a PhD student in Physics, much of my time is spent thinking about research, equations, and the universe. Football offers a different kind of excitement: the drama of a match, the joy of a great goal, the heartbreak of a defeat, and the feeling of being part of something bigger than yourself.

My two favourite clubs are Manchester United and Mohun Bagan. They represent two different football cultures, but both inspire the same passion and loyalty.

The clubs I support

The Red Devils

Manchester United

A club with a remarkable history, legendary players, dramatic comebacks, and a global community of supporters. Supporting Manchester United means standing by the club through its highs and lows and celebrating the moments that make football unforgettable.

Mohun Bagan

Mohun Bagan is much more than a football club. Its long history, connection with Kolkata, and place in Indian football make it a source of immense pride. The green-and-maroon colours represent a sporting tradition cherished by generations of supporters.

A lifelong inspiration

Cristiano Ronaldo

Cristiano Ronaldo has been a motivation for me since his early days. His journey, relentless discipline, dedication to training, and determination to keep improving have inspired me for years.

What I admire most is the mindset: setting ambitious goals, working tirelessly towards them, learning from setbacks, and refusing to stop striving for excellence. His story reminds me that talent matters, but consistency, resilience, and hard work matter just as much.

For me, Ronaldo is more than a footballing icon; he is a lifelong source of motivation to keep pushing forward, both on and off the pitch.
