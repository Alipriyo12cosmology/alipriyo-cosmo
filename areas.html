Research Interests

  • Origin and evolution of primordial perturbations
    • Computation of inflationary power spectra and higher-order correlation functions
    • Comparison of inflationary models with cosmological data
    • Constraining inflationary models using machine learning algorithms
  • Generation and detection of primordial gravitational waves (GWs)
    • Spectral shape of primary GWs at high frequencies
    • Scalar-induced GWs in ultra-slow-roll inflation
    • Constraining non-Gaussianities associated with primordial GWs
  • Observational constraints on (p)reheating
    • Effects of (p)reheating on primordial GWs
    • Formation of primordial black holes (PBHs) from preheating instabilities
    • Mass distribution of PBHs and complementary observables
